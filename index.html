05 / explain the why

Decisions worth remembering

These are the short answers to the questions most likely to come up in a review.

Why standardize mileage?

Raw mileages are large numbers. Standardization keeps gradient descent numerically stable, then the parameters are converted back before persistence.

normalized_km = (km - mean_km) / std_km
What does the public model store?

Only the original-scale parameters are persisted. Prediction does not need to know that training used normalization internally.

estimatePrice(km) = theta0 + theta1 * km
Why MAE and MSE?

MAE is easy to interpret in price units. MSE penalizes large errors more heavily. Lower is better; zero is perfect.

MAE = mean(abs(prediction - actual))
MSE = mean((prediction - actual)^2)
What does diagnostics measure?

It exposes the already-recorded training cost over time, plus configuration and normalization metadata. It does not change the gradient descent loop.

cost_history[0] = initial cost
cost_history[-1] = final cost
Why keep Bonus separate?

The Mandatory programs stay small and dependency-free. SVG, precision, and diagnostics consume the public model but never become requirements for training or prediction.

What is checked for invalid input?

Missing files, malformed CSV rows, non-numeric or non-finite values, invalid model JSON, zero mileage variance, and unstable training parameters are rejected explicitly.

06 / finish strong

Evaluation checklist

Use this as a final mental checklist. The detailed command scripts live in the repository.

Mandatory

  • Show make verify passing.
  • Explain the public hypothesis.
  • Show simultaneous gradient updates.
  • Explain internal normalization and conversion.
  • Train, inspect theta.json, and predict.
  • Demonstrate validation errors.

Bonus

  • Generate and open the SVG plot.
  • Point out the original-scale regression line.
  • Run MAE and MSE.
  • Export diagnostics and inspect cost history.
  • Explain that no external packages are required.
  • Keep Bonus tools independent from Mandatory.
